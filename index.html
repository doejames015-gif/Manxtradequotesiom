About Manx Trade Quotes

Helping local projects find local trades

Manx Trade Quotes is a proposed independent lead-generation service, launching with driveway and groundworks enquiries across the Isle of Man.

For customers

Describe your project once and request no-obligation quotes from suitable independent contractors.

For contractors

Receive enquiries aligned with the type of work and locations your business covers, subject to agreed terms.

Independent platform

We do not perform construction work or guarantee contractor availability, prices or workmanship.

Frequently asked questions

Is requesting a quote free?

Yes. The planned service will be free for customers to request quotes.

Am I obliged to accept a quote?

No. You decide whether to engage any contractor.

Are contractors employed by Manx Trade Quotes?

No. Contractors are independent businesses. Their terms, quotations and work are their own responsibility.

Is the website live?

No. This is an interactive prototype. Demo forms do not send information to Manx Trade Quotes or any contractor.
